High Performance Computing Resources

NERSC Allocation

Through the DOE Mission Science program, the Molecular Foundry receives a yearly allocation of computing time at NERSC (National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center), the DOE Office of Science supercomputing center at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. You can request NERSC access in your Molecular Foundry user proposal. For questions about NERSC sub-allocations, contact Edward Barnard or David Prendergast (Facility Director, Theory of Nanostructured Materials).

If you use NERSC resources during your user project in 2024 please acknowledge NERSC in your publications of work resulting from the use of NERSC resources:

"This research used resources of the National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center, a DOE Office of Science User Facility supported by the Office of Science of the U.S. Department of Energy under Contract No. DE-AC02-05CH11231 using NERSC award BES-ERCAP0028035."
